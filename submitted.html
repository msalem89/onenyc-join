What to prepare before the call

You do not need a finished packet to sign up. Gather what you have; gaps can often be fixed before the deadline. If a document is missing, we say so on the call rather than guessing.

Group 01

Legal standing

  • NYS Certificate of Incorporation
  • IRS 501(c)(3) Determination Letter
  • IRS EIN Assignment Notice
  • CHAR410 or CHAR500 filing
Group 02

Financial records

  • Current annual operating budget
  • Form 990, 990-EZ, or 990-N
  • Audited or reviewed financials
  • Program line-item budget
Group 03

Board governance

  • Board roster with affiliations
  • Organizational by-laws
  • Conflict of interest policy
  • Board authorization resolution
Group 04

City & insurance

  • General liability certificate
  • Workers’ compensation proof
  • Council disclosure schedules
  • Vendor good-standing check
Two things worth doing today. Check that your NYS charity registration is active and not lapsed — it is the most common hold-up. Then confirm your board can sign an authorisation resolution at short notice; the Council filing cannot go in without it.
