Good to Know · Common Questions

Questions before you sign up

Most neighborhood non-profits do not get disqualified on the merit of their programs. They get held up by an expired state registration, a missing board resolution, or an unverified vendor record. We pre-audit your packet before it reaches the Council.
We serve community organizations across all five boroughs and all 51 New York City Council districts. You apply in the district where your programs serve residents — not where you live or where your office is located. If your programs serve several districts, one application covers them all.
Sign up before November 15 even if a renewal or filing is still in progress. Because the City Council application window opens in January, starting in November gives our team time to identify what is missing and help you cure expired registrations before submission.
