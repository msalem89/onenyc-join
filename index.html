Are You Ready to Apply?

What your organization needs for OneNYC to help you apply for NYC City Council discretionary funding.

Salaam (peace be with you). OneNYC helps community organizations prepare strong applications for New York City Council discretionary funding, which opens each January. Most first awards are $10,000 to $20,000 for a local program. This requirements sheet lists what you need to qualify for our help. If you're missing something, talk to us anyway: many gaps can be fixed before the deadline.

1

Your organization must have

1
Nonprofit incorporation
Incorporated as a not-for-profit. 501(c)(3) is most common; some other 501(c) types may also qualify.
2
Its own EIN
A federal tax ID in your organization's own name. The Council does not allow fiscal sponsors or using another group's EIN.
3
Charities Bureau registration
Current registration with the NYS Attorney General's Charities Bureau, with annual filings up to date, or a documented exemption.
4
An authorized officer
A board member or executive who can sign for the organization and get a form notarized.
5
A clean record
No unresolved problems with City agencies, and willingness to disclose past Council funding and any conflicts of interest.
2

Your program must be

City funding pays for a public service, not for an organization's religious or member activities. Masajid and faith-based groups are welcome to apply for programs that serve the whole community.

ELIGIBLE PROGRAMS
  • Free to participants
  • Open to the public, including non-members
  • Tutoring, homework help, ESL
  • Youth sports and mentoring (outside school hours)
  • Food access, health, senior services
  • Arts and culture open to all
NOT ELIGIBLE
  • Worship or prayer services
  • Religious instruction (e.g. Quran classes)
  • Members-only activities
  • Anything that promotes a religion
  • Political activity, campaigns or lobbying
  • Programs during school hours, unless arranged through the DOE with the principal's consent
A school or program under your masjid? If it uses the masjid's EIN, it can't apply on its own: either the masjid applies for a clearly public program it runs, or the school incorporates and gets its own EIN. We'll help you choose.

Good to know: funding limits for newer organizations

IF YOUR ORGANIZATION... CITY LIMIT
Was legally created within the last two fiscal years Up to $10,000 per Council Member and $20,000 total. No subcontractors.
Has not received Council funding in the last three fiscal years Up to $25,000 per Council Member and $50,000 total.

These are limits, not bans, and the Council can waive them case by case (for example, a program that recently became independent from a parent organization). Arts and culture programs funded through the Department of Cultural Affairs have extra eligibility rules; we'll check which route fits you.
