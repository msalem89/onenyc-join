The Complete Dossier

Thirteen documents. One pre-cleared filing.

We assemble, pre-audit, and format all 13 required filings once. Then we submit directly to the Council Member who represents your neighborhood.

Legal

4 papers

  • IRS 501(c)(3) Determination Letter
  • NYS Certificate of Incorporation
  • Organizational By-Laws
  • NYS Charities Registration (CHAR410 / CHAR500)

Financial

4 papers

  • IRS Form 990 / 990-EZ Annual Return
  • NYS CHAR500 Annual Filing Schedule
  • Certified Independent Financial Audit / Review
  • Board-Approved Current Operating Budget

Governance

3 papers

  • Current Board of Directors Roster
  • Adopted Conflict of Interest Policy
  • Signed Board Authorization Resolution

Insurance

2 papers

  • Commercial General Liability (CGL) Certificate
  • Federal EIN Assignment Letter (CP 575)

Common Questions

Before you apply.

Which district do I apply in?

The district your programs serve. Not where you live. Not where your office sits. Serve several districts? One application covers them all.

Is the first award the ceiling?

No. Funding renews yearly, so a funded project can request more the following January. The first award is not the ceiling.

What does it cost?

Zero fee. No payment at any stage. We are a civic non-profit coalition. You keep every dollar of the award.
