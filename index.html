2 Stop 02 • The Reality

The paperwork takes longer than the program.

Most first applications do not fail on program merits. They fail on technical compliance: a missing board resolution, an uncertified financial review, an unfiled CHAR500, or a formatting error on the municipal schedule. When one paper is missing, the entire application is disqualified before your Council Member ever reads it.
