It applies where documents the Organization shares with OneNYC contain Protected Health Information ("PHI") as defined by HIPAA, 45 C.F.R. Parts 160 and 164. OneNYC performs document intake and processing (receipt, review, organization, and preparation support) on the Organization's behalf. By checking the box below and submitting this form, you execute this agreement electronically; the timestamp and your confirmation code are retained as the executed record.

  1. Permitted uses. OneNYC may create, receive, maintain, or transmit PHI only to provide document intake and processing support for the Organization's funding and compliance work, and as required by law. OneNYC will not use or disclose PHI for any other purpose.
  2. Safeguards. OneNYC will apply administrative, physical, and technical safeguards that reasonably protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of PHI, including encryption of PHI in transit and at rest, and access limited to workforce members who need it to perform the services.
  3. Minimum necessary. OneNYC will limit uses and disclosures of PHI to the minimum necessary for the services, except where HIPAA permits or requires otherwise.
  4. Misuse reporting. OneNYC will report to the Organization any use or disclosure of PHI not permitted by this BAA, and any security incident of which it becomes aware, without unreasonable delay.
  5. Subcontractors (service providers). OneNYC will bind any subcontractor or service provider that creates, receives, maintains, or transmits PHI on its behalf to written obligations at least as protective as this BAA, and remains responsible for their compliance.
  6. Individual rights. OneNYC will make PHI available to the Organization when needed for an individual's access, amendment, or accounting-of-disclosures request, and will provide information the Organization reasonably needs to fulfill its duties under HIPAA.
  7. Government access. OneNYC will make its internal practices and records relating to PHI available to the U.S. Secretary of Health and Human Services for purposes of determining HIPAA compliance.
  8. Breach notification. OneNYC will notify the Organization without unreasonable delay, and no later than five (5) business days after discovering, any breach of unsecured PHI, and will provide the details the Organization needs to meet its own notification obligations.
  9. Termination and return. The Organization may terminate this BAA on written notice if OneNYC materially breaches it and fails to cure within thirty (30) days. On termination or at the end of the engagement, OneNYC will return or destroy all PHI; if return or destruction is infeasible, protections continue for as long as PHI is retained.
  10. Governing law. This BAA is governed by the laws of the State of New York; where HIPAA and its regulations apply, they control in case of conflict.
