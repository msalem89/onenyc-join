2. What we do not collect

We do not ask about religion or religious affiliation. We do not store passwords for any external portal — PASSPort and NYC.ID accounts always remain under your organization's control.

3. How we use it

To respond to your registration, record your click-through acceptance of the Indemnity & Disclaimer, grant workspace access after review signed, and support your PASSPort enrollment if you request it. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with funders or other organizations.

4. Where it lives and who can see it

Registration records are stored on infrastructure controlled by OneNYC, outside the public web root. Access is limited to staff working on your onboarding. Documents are shared through a private workspace restricted to your organization and the OneNYC team.

5. How long we keep it

Registration and engagement records are kept for the duration of the engagement and for as long as needed to meet record-keeping obligations afterwards. You can ask us to delete your registration when there is no such obligation.

6. Your choices

Write to info@onenyc.com to access, correct, or delete your information, or to withdraw from the program.
